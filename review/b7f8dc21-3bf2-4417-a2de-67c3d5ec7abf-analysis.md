This playbook provides a focused approach toward the specific decisions that must be made 
when setting up an observability framework from scratch or modernizing an existing orga-
nizational system. Each play aligns specific data collected and retained with its intended 
audience and purpose, highlighting areas where the business can capture value based on the 
guidance provided.
A large number of systems and services is not a barrier to adopting an effective observability 
framework. Conversely, greater complexity increases the need for a well-defined observability 
framework to prevent visibility gaps along business-critical verticals.
	– Develop an observability framework that sustainably enables key business strategy
	– Decrease time to realized value by standardizing and surfacing core metrics (Play 1)
	– Improve availability and IT uptime with a proactive monitoring strategy (Play 2)
	– Reduce operational costs for observability with a well-defined tagging structure and 
efficient FTE allocation for implementation (Play 2)
	– Decrease MTTD/R and resources allocated to firefighting incidents with precise, effective 
alerts, resulting in reduced operational costs and protection against customer revenue loss 
(Plays 3 & 4)
	– Manage risk with maintainable, continuous improvement processes (Next Steps)
